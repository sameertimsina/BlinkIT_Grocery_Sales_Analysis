# Blinkit Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores Blinkit's sales data to understand product performance, outlet characteristics, and factors associated with sales. Python and data analysis libraries were used to clean the data, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualize trends, and develop business recommendations.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across product categories.
- Compare outlet types, sizes, and location tiers.
- Identify top- and low-performing product categories and outlets.
- Examine relationships between item visibility, weight, ratings, and sales.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Exploration:** Examined the dataset structure, columns, and basic statistics.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked for missing values and duplicates and standardized inconsistent category labels.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyzed product categories, outlet characteristics, sales, and ratings.
4. **Data Visualization:** Created charts to compare product and outlet performance.
5. **Business Recommendations:** Identified opportunities to improve sales and inventory planning.

## Key Findings
- The dataset contains **8,523 records**.
- Total sales were approximately **1.20 million** in the dataset's sales units.
- Fruits and Vegetables generated the highest sales among product categories, followed by Snack Foods.
- Supermarket Type1 generated the highest total sales among outlet types.
- Tier 3 locations recorded the highest total sales among the three location tiers.
- Item Visibility, Item Weight, and Rating had very weak linear correlations with Sales in this dataset.

## Business Recommendations
- Prioritize inventory planning for high-performing product categories.
- Investigate low-performing categories to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Review the performance of lower-sales outlets to identify potential operational issues.
- Use outlet-level and location-level sales comparisons to support business decisions.
- Monitor sales and product performance regularly to guide inventory and promotional strategies.

## Conclusion
This analysis provides an overview of product and outlet sales performance. The findings can help guide inventory planning, outlet evaluation, and data-driven business decisions.
